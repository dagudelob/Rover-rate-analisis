Data Science Academy

Interactive examples of the techniques used in this platform
01

IQR Outlier Detection

The Interquartile Range (IQR) identifies extreme price points that distort your statistical benchmarks. Any price below Q1 - 1.5×IQR or above Q3 + 1.5×IQR is classified as an outlier.

IQR = Q3 - Q1  |  Bounds: [Q1 - 1.5·IQR,  Q3 + 1.5·IQR]
📌 Used in this app to auto-flag sitters with abnormal rates in the Outlier Studio.
02

Revenue Maximizer (EVI)

The Expected Value / Revenue Index finds the price that maximizes: Price × P(Being hired at that price). Higher prices earn more per job but reduce your booking probability.

EVI(P) = P × P(hired | P)  |  P(hired) modeled via logistic curve on market distribution
📌 Used in this app to compute the recommended pricing sweet spot in the Price Optimizer section.
03

Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)

KDE estimates the smooth continuous probability distribution of market prices — unlike a histogram, it doesn't depend on arbitrary bin widths. It reveals hidden clusters (e.g. "budget" vs. "premium" sitters in the same area).

f̂(x) = (1/n·h) · Σ K((x − xᵢ)/h)  |  K = Gaussian kernel, h = bandwidth
📌 Extension: Replace the histogram in Market Charts with a KDE curve for smoother density visualization.
04

K-Means Price Clustering

Groups sitters into K price clusters (e.g. Budget / Mid-tier / Premium) using their rates. The algorithm iteratively reassigns each sitter to the nearest centroid, revealing natural market segments in your area.

Minimize: Σᵢ Σⱼ ||xᵢ - μⱼ||²  |  μⱼ = cluster centroid (mean price)
📌 Extension: Identify whether your local market is truly segmented and position yourself in the highest-value cluster.
