5-Service Pricing Strategy & Margin Multipliers

Cross-service unit economics & elasticity benchmarks

Dog Walking

Base Anchor (1.0×)

Unit: 30 or 60 min walk • Elasticity: High ($|\epsilon| > 1.2$)

Benchmark Rate: $20 - $30

🎯 Strategy: Volume & Route Density. Keep base price competitive with market median, and cluster walks geographically in a 1.5km zone to minimize unpaid commute transit.

🏠

Drop-In Visits

0.9× – 1.1× Base

Unit: 30 min home check-in • Elasticity: Moderate ($|\epsilon| \approx 1.0$)

Benchmark Rate: $18 - $28

🎯 Strategy: Multi-Pet Upsell. Base rate matches walking, but add-on pets (+$10-15/cat or extra dog) drive 80%+ profit margins with near-zero additional travel overhead.

🏡

Overnight Boarding

1.8× – 2.5× Base

Unit: Per night (in your home) • Elasticity: Low ($|\epsilon| < 0.8$)

Benchmark Rate: $45 - $75

🎯 Strategy: Capacity Monetization. Boarding is limited by home capacity (1-2 pets). Price high with premium trust signaling and enforce a +35% holiday surge multiplier.

🛋️

House Sitting

2.2× – 3.2× Base

Unit: Per night (in owner's home) • Elasticity: Very Inelastic ($|\epsilon| < 0.6$)

Benchmark Rate: $60 - $100+

🎯 Strategy: Time Exclusivity Premium. Because you cannot take overlapping house sits, charge top-dollar. Ideal for digital nomads and remote professionals who work from the client's home.

☀️

Doggy Day Care

1.4× – 1.9× Base

Unit: Full Day (8am - 6pm) • Elasticity: Moderate ($|\epsilon| \approx 1.0$)

Benchmark Rate: $32 - $50

🎯 Strategy: Recurring Retention. Offer 5-day / month-long pass bundles. Predictable recurring cashflow offsets the slight per-day discount vs. one-off drop-ins.

Data Science Academy

Interactive examples of the techniques used in this platform
01

IQR Outlier Detection

The Interquartile Range (IQR) identifies extreme price points that distort your statistical benchmarks. Any price below $Q_1 - 1.5\cdot\text{IQR}$ or above $Q_3 + 1.5\cdot\text{IQR}$ is classified as an outlier.

$$\text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1 \quad \Big| \quad \text{Bounds: } \left[ Q_1 - 1.5\cdot\text{IQR}, \; Q_3 + 1.5\cdot\text{IQR} \right]$$
📌 Used in this app to auto-flag sitters with abnormal rates in the Outlier Studio.
02

Revenue Maximizer (EVI)

The Expected Value / Revenue Index finds the price that maximizes: $\text{EVI}(P) = P \times \mathbb{P}(\text{hired} \mid P)$. Higher prices earn more per job but reduce booking probability.

$$\text{EVI}(P) = P \times \mathbb{P}(\text{hired} \mid P) \quad \Big| \quad \mathbb{P}(\text{hired} \mid P) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \frac{1}{1 + e^{(P - P_i)/h}}$$
📌 Used in this app to compute the recommended pricing sweet spot in the Price Optimizer section.
03

Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)

KDE estimates the smooth continuous probability distribution of market prices without arbitrary bin intervals. It reveals bimodal density clusters (e.g. budget vs. premium sitters).

$$\hat{f}_h(x) = \frac{1}{nh} \sum_{i=1}^n K\left(\frac{x - x_i}{h}\right) \quad \Big| \quad K(u) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{1}{2}u^2}, \; h = 1.06\hat{\sigma}n^{-1/5}$$
📌 Extension: Replace the histogram in Market Charts with a KDE curve for smoother density visualization.
04

K-Means Price Clustering

Groups sitters into $K$ natural price clusters (e.g. Budget / Mid-tier / Premium) by iteratively minimizing within-cluster sum of squared distances to centroids.

$$\arg\min_{\mathbf{S}} \sum_{i=1}^k \sum_{\mathbf{x} \in S_i} \|\mathbf{x} - \boldsymbol{\mu}_i\|^2 \quad \Big| \quad \boldsymbol{\mu}_i = \frac{1}{|S_i|}\sum_{\mathbf{x} \in S_i} \mathbf{x}$$
📌 Extension: Identify whether your local market is truly segmented and position yourself in the highest-value cluster.

Data Science & Econometrics Encyclopedia

Technical foundations, formulas & Wikipedia citations

Empirical Survival Function (ESF) ↗

Econometrics

In market economics, the survival function $S(P) = \mathbb{P}(\text{Price} \ge P)$ models the probability that a buyer chooses a given seller over available market alternatives. In this app, we compute an empirical, continuous survival curve smoothed via Gaussian kernels to estimate hiring probability at every candidate price point.

Related Wikipedia articles: Kaplan–Meier Estimator • Complementary CDF

Price Elasticity of Demand (PED) ↗

Microeconomics

Measures the responsiveness of booking quantity/probability to a change in price: $\epsilon = \frac{\% \Delta Q}{\% \Delta P} = \frac{P}{Q}\frac{dQ}{dP}$. When $|\epsilon| < 1$ (inelastic), price increases boost revenue. When $|\epsilon| > 1$ (elastic), price increases reduce revenue. Total expected revenue is maximized at the Unitary Elasticity Point ($|\epsilon| = 1.0$).

Related Wikipedia articles: Marginal Revenue • Total Revenue Test

Interquartile Range (IQR) & Outlier Fences ↗

Descriptive Statistics

A non-parametric measure of statistical dispersion representing the spread of the middle 50% of values: $\text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1$. John Tukey defined outlier fences at $[Q_1 - 1.5\cdot\text{IQR}, \; Q_3 + 1.5\cdot\text{IQR}]$, which we apply in the Outlier Studio to flag distorted price rates without making normal distribution assumptions.

Related Wikipedia articles: Tukey's Fences • Box Plots

Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) ↗

Non-Parametric Estimation

A statistical technique for estimating the continuous probability density function (PDF) of price variables. Unlike discrete histograms that depend on arbitrary bin intervals, KDE applies smooth Gaussian kernels $\hat{f}_h(x) = \frac{1}{nh}\sum K\left(\frac{x-x_i}{h}\right)$ using Silverman's rule of thumb bandwidth $h = 1.06\hat{\sigma}n^{-1/5}$.

Related Wikipedia articles: Silverman's Bandwidth • Gaussian Kernel

Trimmed Mean (Truncated Mean) ↗

Robust Statistics

A statistical measure of central tendency calculated by discarding a fixed percentage (e.g. 10%) of the lowest and highest values before computing the arithmetic average. It offers a balance between the median (highly robust but loses magnitude info) and the mean (vulnerable to severe skew).

Related Wikipedia articles: Robust Statistics • Winsorized Mean

Server-Sent Events (SSE) ↗

Web Engineering

A W3C standard protocol enabling servers to push asynchronous, unidirectional text event streams to web clients over HTTP (`text/event-stream`). Used in this platform to stream live Playwright scraping logs and page-by-page progress in real-time without polling overhead.

Related Wikipedia articles: WebSockets • HTTP Push Technology
